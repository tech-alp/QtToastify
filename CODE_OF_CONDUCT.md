# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

QtToastify aims to provide a professional, welcoming, and safe collaboration
space for users, contributors, and maintainers.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate respectfully and focus feedback on the work.
- Welcome different experience levels, backgrounds, and viewpoints.
- Give and receive technical criticism constructively.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.
- Protect private information shared during collaboration.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith interaction.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Commercial promotion or spam unrelated to the project.

## Scope

This policy applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, and other spaces where someone represents QtToastify.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report possible violations privately to `enesalp1313@gmail.com` with the subject
`QtToastify Code of Conduct`. Do not disclose sensitive incident details in a
public issue.

Maintainers will review reports privately and consider the safety and context of
everyone involved. Depending on severity and recurrence, actions may include a
private warning, temporary participation limits, suspension, or a permanent ban.
Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/)
